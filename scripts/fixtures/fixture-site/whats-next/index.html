Plans

What's next

What we're working on and thinking about. These are plans, not promises, and they carry no dates. Plans change as we learn. What already works is on the build page.

In the works

  • Custom roles that take orders on the register.
  • Staff sales that follow a check when it’s handed to someone else.
  • “My checks”: each person’s own checks, handed over cleanly when a shift changes.
  • Seats shown on the kitchen screen.
  • A notification center.

Coming

  • Approvals from a manager’s phone.
  • Kitchen alerts when food is ready.
  • Sizes and price levels.
  • Ticket print options: an expo ticket, and a “goes with” line on kitchen tickets.

Next

The next big features, in the order we plan to build them. The first three go together.

  1. Seats on every device, and kitchen tickets in seat order
  2. Courses, with hold and fire
  3. Split checks, and paying one check with more than one tender
  4. Tableside that works the same way as the register
  5. Tax exemptions and service fees

Planned, not scheduled yet

  • Card payments
  • A live view of the tip pool, so everyone in it sees their share
  • Reasons recorded on voids, cancels and discounts
  • Finer permissions for each setup screen
  • More report screens, and reports and exports that always agree

Ideas

  • Two devices working on one check at once
  • Connections to accounting tools and to delivery orders
  • Receipt templates
  • Easier setup: staff import and a go-live checklist
  • Labor rules
